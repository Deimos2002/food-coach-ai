Food Coach playground

A weekly AI food coach that may only say what its data supports. Pick a sample week: the backend has already computed the facts; the model words the advice from retrieved WHO guidance, and a deterministic checker accepts or rejects it. All weeks are synthetic.

Measured on 24 Nov 2026 over 41 held-out weeks: all 39 weeks sent to the model got a review that passed the checker (30 on the first try, the rest on the retry), and the checker rejected 20 of 20 planted bad outputs. Run files
